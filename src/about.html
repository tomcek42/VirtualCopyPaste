Virtual Copy Paste

Type text character-by-character into VMs and remote sessions where clipboard paste doesn't work.

Keyboard Modes

Standard (Unicode)

✔ All characters including special symbols and emoji

✘ May not work in deeply nested VDI/remote sessions

Compatible (VKey)

✔ Works in nested remote sessions (Horizon → vCenter → VM)

✘ Only characters available on your keyboard layout
