30 ms
Delay between modifier keys (Shift/Ctrl) and character keys. Increase if uppercase/special characters are wrong in nested remote sessions (0–50 ms)
For targets that indent automatically (code editors, YAML/JSON views). After each line break the indentation the target added is selected, so your own indentation overwrites it instead of stacking on top. Nothing is deleted. Turn off for text consoles without keyboard selection (Linux TTY, vi over SSH), where Home only moves the cursor.
